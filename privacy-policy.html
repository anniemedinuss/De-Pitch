Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 January, 2026

Dé Pitch (“we”, “our”, “us”) is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information when you interact with our website, services, and platforms.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following information:

  • Personal details (name, email address, phone number)
  • Professional information (CVs, employment history, career interests)
  • Payment and billing information
  • Communications sent to us (emails, forms, consultations)
  • Website usage data (IP address, browser type, pages visited)

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Deliver our services (career consulting, recruitment, human capital management)
  • Communicate with you about services, updates, or support
  • Improve our website and offerings
  • Process payments and bookings
  • Comply with legal obligations

3. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal data. We may share information with:

  • Trusted service providers (payment processors, scheduling tools)
  • Hiring partners (only with your consent)
  • Legal or regulatory authorities where required by law

4. Data Storage & Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data. However, no online system is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Request corrections or deletion
  • Withdraw consent at any time
  • Request how your data is being used

To exercise these rights, contact us at office@depitchhq.com.

6. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party platforms. We are not responsible for their privacy practices.

International Data Transfers

Where personal data is transferred outside Nigeria or the European Economic Area (EEA), dé pitch ensures that appropriate safeguards are in place, including:

  • Secure data storage systems
  • Trusted third-party service providers
  • Compliance with international data protection standards

7. Updates

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.
