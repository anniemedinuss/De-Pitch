Origin

Built on belief in purposeful work

De Pitch emerged from a simple observation: most people spend their lives in jobs that don't fit. We started because we believed employment could be different, more human, more real.

  • Founders who lived the struggle
  • A team that understands career transitions

A note from our founder

Three paths, one purpose

Whether you’re climbing the corporate ladder, building your own venture, or navigating the gig economy, we meet you where you are. At Dé pitch, we believe work should never be a one-size-fits-all box. Your career, your craft, and your ambition deserve room to evolve.

Your work should fit your life not the other way around. And however you choose to show up in the world of work, we’re here to help you pitch it with clarity, confidence, and purpose.
