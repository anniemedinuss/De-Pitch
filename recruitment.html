Questions

Everything you need to know about building lasting talent strategies with De Pitch.

What makes De Pitch different?

We believe recruitment is about matching people to work that matters, not just filling roles quickly.

Do you offer one-time placements?

Yes, we provide single placements as well as one-year and lifetime retainership options depending on your needs.

How long does the process take?

Timeline varies based on the role and your requirements, but we prioritize quality matches over speed.

Can you help with senior-level positions?

We work across all levels, from entry positions to executive roles, with the same commitment to fit.

What's included in retainership?

Retainership means ongoing support, replacement guarantees, and continued partnership beyond the initial placement.

Do you work with freelancers?

We support self-employment and contract work through our Self-Employment Support services as well.
