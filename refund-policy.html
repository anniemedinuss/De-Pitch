Refund Policy

Last updated: 15 January, 2026

We value transparency and fairness. Please review our refund policy carefully.

1. Digital & Consulting Services

Due to the nature of our services:

  • Completed services are non-refundable
  • Partially delivered services are non-refundable
  • Retainer agreements are non-refundable once activated

2. Eligible Refunds

Refunds may be considered only if:

  • Payment was made in error (duplicate payment)
  • A service was not delivered due to fault solely on our end

3. Non-Refundable Scenarios

No refunds will be issued for:

  • Change of mind
  • Missed sessions without notice
  • Failure to provide required information
  • Dissatisfaction based on outcome expectations

4. Refund Requests

Requests must be submitted within 7 days of payment to office@depitchhq.com with proof of payment. Approved refunds will be processed within 24 hours–14 business days.
